# FAQ

**Q: Why is VODER not an app that I can install?**

A: VODER is not a product by its own — it's a kernel/core that can be a SaaS for someone else.

**Q: Why is VODER's GUI ignored and poorly gets updates?**

A: Because I am focused on the core itself so someone else could steal (take) it and slap an UI and billing and go.

**Q: If VODER is a core, why are the models hardcoded?**

A: Because they are the SOTA and anyone would end up with these models.

**Q: Is this project inspired by ElevenLabs?**

A: Not directly, but anyone would make a chatbot/LLM would of course have heard about ChatGPT. I have mentioned the origin of the project in [Guide.md](Guide.md) (About VODER).

**Q: Is this a reverse-engineered ElevenLabs?**

A: No, another audio platform does not mean another ElevenLabs.

**Q: Why?**

A: Because I am aiming to give developers the full pre-built core to use as a backbone.

**Q: Why are there too many features instead of focusing on one thing?**

A: It's not bloating — it's just aiming to be an open-sourced unified audio platform.

**Q: Will it be a website?**

A: No, maybe someone makes their own website on top of it.

**Q: Why?**

A: Because I just build the core.

**Q: It does not work on my machine?**

A: It works on mine.

**Q: Will this project keep developing?**

A: It will always be on the cutting-edge tech following the AI audio development.

**Q: Does this project offer something exclusive or brand-new?**

A: No, it's just an open-source audio platform.

**Q: So it's just someone else's work?**

A: And someone else's work was built on someone else's previous work btw.

**Q: Does this work completely offline?**

A: You are absolutely right! It works offline using your hardware once you have the required models. What else can I help you with today? If you want to brainstorm, craft ideas, or just argue, I am always here and ready.

**Q: Are you a human?**

A: No, I am an open-source audio platform project.
